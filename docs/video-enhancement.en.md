# Video enhancement with AI MediaKit

[README](../README.en.md) · [中文](video-enhancement.md)

1. Under **Video enhancement** in Settings, add a Key with AI MediaKit permission, verify pricing and enable it. The creation assistant and a tool-capable assistant model must also be enabled. Enhancement has its own configuration, separate from image/video generation. Runtime credentials, endpoints and prices come from the saved model configuration; production does not require the `MEDIA_KIT_KEY` environment variable.
2. The storyboard retains its **Enhance** action. On the infinite canvas, drag a video output to empty space and choose **HD video**. The new card uses the dark canvas surface with centered resolution and enhancement actions; service selection stays in its selected-node toolbar. After cost confirmation it shows progress and the completed video, with no intermediate converter or export dialog. Once submitted, its source, service and resolution stay locked, including through undo and connection editing. Delete the card or drag a new connection to change parameters; deleting a canvas card does not delete the saved video. Other shots do not need to be finished. Complete episode exports remain on the video page.
3. Resolution can only increase, using actual file dimensions: equal targets and downgrades are rejected. Canvas targets are the enabled, priced 720p, 1080p, 2K and 4K tiers. Existing configurations retain their 1080p tier; edit the service in Settings to enable and price additional tiers. The canvas lists all supported targets and distinguishes disabled service tiers from targets that do not exceed the source; showing these options never changes prices or enables a tier automatically. MediaKit generative enhancement accepts inputs up to 1080p, so 1080p→2K/4K is supported while 2K output cannot be submitted again. Generate 4K directly from an eligible source instead. A successful clip enhancement becomes the current shot video for preview, download and composition; the original stays in video history. If the selection changed meanwhile or a higher-resolution result from the same source is already selected, the enhancement is kept in history without replacing that selection. Existing completed enhancements can also be selected with “Use as current video”, without another generation or charge. Whole-episode exports remain separate artifacts.
4. Check the frozen version, target resolution, actual duration and quote before confirming enhancement. Aspect ratio and frame rate are preserved. Source and HD files remain separate; matching successful results are reused without another enhancement charge. Chat uses the same tools and requires confirmation of the specific export and quote.
5. Background collection continues after the browser closes. A download failure retries saving the existing result; an uncertain submission requires review rather than another paid submission. Prepare a new source version after changing shots.

Enhancement has its own usage records, based on reported duration, output specifications and configured prices. Quotes and application records are not independently verified provider invoices. Missing usage is flagged for review rather than treated as verified zero cost. The application checks output dimensions, duration and frame rate and preserves the source audio. Evaluate quality and identity consistency on your actual footage; fixed savings are not guaranteed.

Local exports use `CHAPTER_EXPORT_PATH` (default `./data/chapter-exports`), outside public `MEDIA_PATH`, with project-scoped downloads. The default Docker data volume includes this directory; persist and back up custom paths and never expose them as static files. Local input uses MediaKit temporary uploads, so no public local port is needed. OSS uses existing private storage and signed URLs. Keep credentials out of Skills, task snapshots and source code.

See the [development plan](superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-mediakit-video-enhancement.md) and [execution and acceptance record](superpowers/verification/2026-10-27-mediakit-video-enhancement.md) for contracts, recovery and rollback. Individual generated videos and complete episodes are supported. Canvas video cards offer configured resolution targets, and matching source/service/target combinations reuse tasks and results. Frame interpolation and automatic per-shot processing remain outside scope. See the [official API](https://docs.volcengine.com/docs/intelligent-processing/enhance-video-generative?lang=en) and [pricing](https://docs.volcengine.com/docs/Intelligentprocessing/video-tool-billing?lang=en).

## Scope and acceptance

When selected clips have different dimensions, composition fits and pads them to the largest selected frame size. This retains HD output dimensions without running AI enhancement on the remaining clips or charging another model call. A promoted HD clip uses ordinary shot-video storage and access; whole-episode exports remain in private export storage.

The UI and capability configuration support 720p, 1080p, 2K and 4K. Real acceptance has covered 1080p and a 2K sample; 4K provider output and long-form quality have not been independently accepted. No fixed savings or equivalence to native high-resolution generation is promised.
