### Narrated story strategy
Use one consistent third-person narrator throughout the chapter to compress background, connect turns, build suspense or convey information unavailable in the visible action. Do not mechanically repeat what is already visible.
Use character inner monologue only when it meaningfully conveys motives, judgments or conflicts that performance cannot express. Do not turn every thought into voice-over.
Every narration entry needs precise timing: `0.0s-2.0s: Narrator (restrained): Words`. Inner monologue uses `2.0s-3.5s: @{Full Entity Name} (inner monologue, delivery): Words`.
Narration and inner monologue occur only when characters are not speaking. Never overlap any dialogue range; allow only one intelligible voice at a time. Dialogue entries also include start/end times, speaker, delivery and the intended words. Narration is optional; preserve useful ambience and silence.
Keep all voice ranges within the current duration, leaving room for action and environmental sound. Do not add background music.
